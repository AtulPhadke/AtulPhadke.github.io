Karma

A team of AI agents that decides which ML jobs run where and when, across a team’s own GPUs and the cloud.

01

What teams do today

Teams decide ahead of time how much compute to buy and which job runs where. Neither changes much when the work does.

The same block every week

Cloud GPUs are cheapest reserved a year ahead, so teams reserve enough for their busiest weeks and pay for it every week. In quiet weeks it sits idle. Before a deadline it still isn’t enough, and jobs wait.

Fixed rules

A scheduler ranks jobs by priority, size, and quota. The rules stay the same while deadlines move, prices change, and racks fill up unevenly.

02

How Karma works

Karma swaps the fixed formula for agents that read the queue, deadlines, a note from the scientist on each job, prices, and what’s already running.

  1. 1

    Coordinators

    A global coordinator routes each job to a cluster, holds the monthly budget, and approves new compute.

  2. 2

    Planners

    One per GPU pool. Only planners place, preempt, or defer jobs, or bring up spot, on-demand, or reserved GPUs.

  3. 3

    Code checks

    Every move is checked against budget, quotas, and all-or-nothing placement before Kueue runs it. If no valid plan comes back, a person gets paged.

Ahead

88% of deadlines met, against 71% for the best baseline, at about 13% less per GPU-hour of finished work. Its stock-out warnings also have 36% lower error than the best simple rule.

Behind

They bought too little compute and finished less than half as much work. Most of it traced to a budget check that refused purchases at about 40% of real spend. It’s fixed, and a three-day rerun is in progress.

One simulated day, not production; runs of the same day can differ by several deadlines. The baselines simulate each tool’s scheduling policy, not the products themselves. The forecaster’s score is the Brier score of 6-hour stock-out warnings on simulated days it never trained on. The agents run on Claude Haiku.

How I’m building it

Part 1 · Matterworks.ai · 2026

A priority scheduler on Kueue

At my co-op I built priority-based job scheduling on Kubernetes with Kueue, across dozens of on-prem GPUs and cloud clusters, so a 10-person ML team could run dozens of GPU jobs a day. The rules worked. The right priority kept changing.

Part 2 · July 2026 to now

Karma

Same foundation, with agents on top: the agent team and its checks, compute across clouds, and a simulator that replays real GPU jobs against four baselines.
