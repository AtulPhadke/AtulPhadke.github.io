MLbroker

Personal project, in progress

ML infra

  1. Part 1 Priority GPU scheduler, Matterworks.ai, 2026
  2. Part 2 MLbroker, now

At Matterworks I built a priority scheduler on Kueue, with preemption, quotas, and priority-driven deployment across dozens of GPUs. The rules worked. The problem was that the right priority changed every week. MLbroker is part two: Claude agents place, preempt, and defer jobs across spot, on-demand, and on-prem GPUs under a spend cap, and every decision passes a deterministic safety check before it runs.
